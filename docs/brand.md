# Creatency brand basics

The short version. The full brand book, with every token, is the [Creatency design system](https://claude.ai/artifact/BEhVu9pbG7HLUUxn3CjqVX) (decision 2).

## Colours

| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Navy | `#1B1F3B` | Header bands, primary buttons, text |
| Mustard gold | `#F2B84B` | Earned only: done, progress, Kee. Never text on white |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Cards, paper |
| Slate grey | `#4A4F6E` | Quiet text, lines |
| Indigo | `#3236A8` | Links and small labels |

## Type

- **Space Grotesk** (500, 700): titles, stage numbers, the tagline.
- **Inter** (400, 600, 700): everything you read.
- Both load free from Google Fonts.

## Logo (placeholder)

Until there's a real logo, the logo is the word **Creatency** in Space Grotesk 700, navy (white on navy), with a 4px gold bar underneath. The tagline goes under it in Space Grotesk 500: **Bring It. Build It. Own It.**

Kee is the companion, not the logo. Never redraw or recolour Kee.

## Voice

Clear, steady and warm. Short sentences, plain words. Say what happens. A paused creation is "Dormant: everything is kept", never "abandoned".

## Village UpKeep

Village UpKeep is its own product with its own look. Its brand basics get written in its own repo, starting from the colours and fonts in the Bubble app.
